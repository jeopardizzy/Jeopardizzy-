## App Review notes (App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes)

> Quizzical Club 1.0.2 adds a free 7-day trial of Full Club, set up as Apple
> describes for non-subscription apps (guideline 3.1.1): a free
> non-consumable in-app purchase named "7-Day Trial" (trial_7_day), submitted
> with this version.
>
> To see it: Trivia Night tab, tap any game marked "🔒 Full Club". The sheet
> shows "Try everything free for 7 days". Before the trial starts, the sheet
> states its length (7 days), that the games and levels listed above it lock
> again afterwards, and that keeping them costs a one-time $7.99 (the Full
> Club purchase). Nothing is charged automatically. The trial can be taken
> once per Apple ID; during it, a "Free trial · N days left" badge opens the
> same sheet, and the last day and the end are each announced once.
>
> Full Club (full_club, $7.99) and "Restore purchase" are unchanged. Nothing
> else in the app changed.
