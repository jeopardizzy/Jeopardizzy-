Full Club

  • What it is: a one-time purchase that unlocks all 18 team games, every workbook level and the "Practice these" drills. No subscription, no ads.
  • Restore on a new device: open the app, tap any locked game (or Workbook → Full Club), then Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with.
  • Family Sharing (iPhone and iPad): Full Club is shared with your family group automatically; family members tap Restore purchase.
  • Refunds: purchases are handled by Apple or Google, so refunds are requested from them — reportaproblem.apple.com for the App Store, or your Google Play order history.

Playing

  • Trivia Night: one device, one host. Pick a game, name 1–4 teams, read each clue aloud, reveal the answer and award the points.
  • Daily Board: one free board a day, the same all day on your device.
  • Workbook: solo practice tests with instant feedback; answer checking forgives small typos.
  • Offline: everything works without an internet connection.

Your progress

Scores and settings live only on your device. To move them to another phone or tablet, use Workbook → Your progress → Back up (file) or Copy to clipboard, then Restore from file or Paste backup text on the new device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
