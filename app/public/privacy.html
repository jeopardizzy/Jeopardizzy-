Quizzical Club

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Quizzical Club collects no personal data. Everything happens on your device; the only exception is the game messages exchanged when you choose to play on several phones, which are end-to-end encrypted and never stored.

Who we are

Quizzical Club is developed and published by GIGVI LLC (Florida, USA). On Google Play it is distributed through the Elyneva Studios developer account. Contact details are at the end of this page.

What the app does

Quizzical Club is an offline trivia and word-game app: team trivia boards, a practice workbook, and guides. It works fully offline after the first load.

Purchases

The app is free to play. An optional one-time purchase, Full Club, unlocks every game. Payment is handled entirely by Apple's App Store or Google Play under their own privacy policies. We never see or receive your payment details — no card number, no billing address, no name. The app only learns whether Full Club has been purchased on your account, so it can unlock the content and restore it on a new device.

Data we collect

  • None. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email addresses.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers.
  • No cookies, no crash-reporting SDKs, no third-party SDKs. The only outside parties involved are Apple and Google, and only for purchases.

The quizmaster voice

The voice that reads clues and answers is made of recordings that ship inside the app. Nothing is generated or downloaded while you play, and no speech service is contacted.

The microphone

The microphone is used only if you choose to answer out loud (Say it) in the Workbook or on a Trivia Night clue, and only while the button says “Listening”, for a few seconds at a time. What you say is checked on your device, in memory, against the expected answer — if needed, by turning it into text on the device — and then discarded, along with that text. Your voice is never recorded, never saved, never placed in any log, and never sent anywhere — the app does not use a cloud speech service. You can always type your answers instead, and you can turn microphone access off at any time in your device's settings.

Playing on several phones

If you choose Play on several phones, the phones in that game exchange short game messages — team names, buzzes, typed answers, scores and the current clue — through a small relay server that we run on Cloudflare. Every message is end-to-end encrypted with a key that exists only in the game's join link and QR code, so the relay (and we) cannot read them. The relay passes messages on in real time and keeps nothing: games exist only in memory while you play and disappear within two hours; nothing is logged or saved, and no accounts or device identifiers are involved. Spoken answers are never sent — only text. If you never use this mode, nothing leaves your device.

What stays on your device

Game scores, team names, practice results, and settings (sound, voice, motion, answer out loud) are stored only in your device's local app storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them permanently. We have no access to this data.

Network use

The app contacts our relay only while you play on several phones (see above). Otherwise, the only network traffic is to Apple's App Store or Google Play, when you buy or restore Full Club or when the app checks that purchase. Content updates are delivered only through app updates from the App Store or Google Play.

Children

The app is family-friendly and safe for all ages. Because nothing is collected, no parental consent mechanics are required.
