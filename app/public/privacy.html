Quizzical Club

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Quizzical Club collects no personal data. Everything happens on your device, and nothing ever leaves it.

What the app does

Quizzical Club is an offline trivia and word-game app: team Jeopardy-style boards, a practice workbook, and guides. It works fully offline after the first load.

Data we collect

  • None. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email addresses.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers.
  • No cookies, no crash-reporting SDKs, no third-party services.

What stays on your device

Game scores, team names, practice results, and settings (sound, motion) are stored only in your device's local app storage. Uninstalling the app deletes them permanently. We have no access to this data.

Network use

The app does not contact any server during normal use. Content updates, if any, are delivered only through app updates from Google Play.

Children

The app is family-friendly and safe for all ages. Because nothing is collected, no parental consent mechanics are required.
